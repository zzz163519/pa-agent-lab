## Scope and method

This is a bounded public-source inventory for the authorized Phase 1 corpus preflight. It records public discovery metadata and content/version hashes for the fetched Markdown representation returned by the read-only Jina Reader fallback, plus public YouTube metadata where separately observed with `yt-dlp`. It is not a full crawl, a legal opinion, a copyright clearance, or a Phase 4 ingestion manifest.

- Primary source surface: Brooks Trading Course public website and the older Brooks Price Action public area.
- First text-oriented extraction surface: public Ask Al Q&A transcript archive.
- Public pages were read through `https://r.jina.ai/<URL>`; no provider, embedding, vector retrieval, training, or RAG service was used.
- Hashes below identify the fetched representation at this snapshot, not an immutable claim about the publisher's future content.
- `copyrightAvailabilityStatus` is an inventory status only. It does not assert legal permission to redistribute or ingest.
- Approximate volume is based on visible archive/index metadata, not a complete crawl.
